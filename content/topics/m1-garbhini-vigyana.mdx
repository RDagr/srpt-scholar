definition:
  ayurveda: >-

    meaning: >-
      Just as flowers bloom in their season, the Artava of a woman flows in its
      proper Ritu Kala. When this natural flow ceases, it is the first
      presumptive sign of Garbha Dharana.
    clinicalNote: >-
      Amenorrhoea remains the earliest and most reliable presumptive sign of

  - source: Charaka Sharira Sthana 4/18

      तत्र दौहृदं नाम— यत्किञ्चिदिच्छति स्त्री गर्भवती तत्सर्वं
      दौहृदमित्युच्यते |

      तस्य अवमानात् कुब्जं खञ्जं जडं वामनं विकृताक्षं वा प्रजायते ||
    transliteration: >-
      tatra dauhṛdaṃ nāma— yatkiñcidiccchati strī garbhavatī tatsarvaṃ
      dauhṛdamityucyate |

      tasya avamānāt kubjaṃ khañjaṃ jaḍaṃ vāmanaṃ vikṛtākṣaṃ vā prajāyate ||
    meaning: >-
      Dauhrida is the term for any desire or craving experienced by a pregnant
      woman. If these desires are ignored, the child may be born with
      deformities.
    clinicalNote: >-
      Dauhrida corresponds to pregnancy cravings in modern obstetrics, often
      attributed to hormonal changes (rising hCG and progesterone).

## Additional Notes

This module is the foundation of Prasuti Tantra studies. Master the classical shlokas on Garbha Lakshana from Charaka Sharira Sthana Chapter 4 — they appear in every university exam.

### Key Classical Texts to Study

1. **Charaka Sharira Sthana** — Chapters 2, 3, 4 (Garbha formation, development, signs)
2. **Sushruta Sharira Sthana** — Chapter 3 (Garbha Vigyana)
3. **Ashtanga Hridaya Sharira** — Chapter 1 (Garbhavakranti)
4. **Kashyapa Samhita** — Khila Sthana (specific details on Dauhrida)

### Recommended Modern Textbooks

1. Williams Obstetrics — Chapters on Implantation, Placentation, Fetal Development
2. DC Dutta's Textbook of Obstetrics — Diagnosis of Pregnancy
3. Mudaliar & Menon — Obstetrics chapter on early pregnancy
